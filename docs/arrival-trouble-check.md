# Arrival Trouble Check

## Idea

Arrival Trouble Check is a future Germany Travel Checker feature for travelers arriving late at night or early in the morning.

It should help visitors avoid the first-day problems that often happen before shops, cafés, pharmacies or hotel check-in options are easy to use.

## User Problem

Travelers may assume they can solve small needs after arrival.

In Germany, late-night or early-morning arrival can make this harder, especially on Sundays or public holidays.

Common trouble:

- arriving tired and hungry
- not having water
- expecting a regular supermarket to be open
- arriving before hotel check-in
- not having cash or coins for toilets, lockers or small places
- needing basic medicine or regular medication
- not knowing whether the arrival date is a Sunday or public holiday
- being unsure whether airport, station or city fallback options are available

## Possible Inputs

- arrival city
- arrival airport or station
- arrival date
- arrival time
- hotel check-in time
- whether the traveler has a transfer day
- selected needs such as water, food, pharmacy, cash or luggage

## Possible Outputs

- Sunday/public holiday warning
- early-morning or late-night arrival warning
- water and snack preparation note
- cash or coins reminder
- hotel check-in gap reminder
- pharmacy caution without medical advice
- live check links for airport, station, supermarkets, cafés, bakeries and pharmacies
- reminder to save hotel address and route before arrival

## Human Tone Direction

The feature should sound like it was made by someone who knows the small travel problems visitors actually face.

Good tone:

- If you land at 5 a.m., do not assume a normal supermarket will be open.
- Bring water and a snack before you are tired, not after.
- If your hotel check-in is much later, plan where you will wait with luggage.
- Keep a payment backup. Some small things may still need cash or coins.

Avoid:

- panic wording
- generic travel blog advice
- pretending that every airport or station option is open
- claiming exact opening hours without live data

## Product Principles

This feature should:

- remain rule-based
- avoid exact opening-hour claims
- avoid live pharmacy duty schedule management
- avoid medical diagnosis or treatment advice
- encourage official or live-source verification
- avoid panic wording
- focus on practical preparation

## Monetization Fit Later

Arrival trouble is a natural future surface for:

- eSIM
- airport transfers
- luggage storage
- travel insurance
- public transport passes
- hotel early check-in guidance

Do not add monetization before the checker is useful and trusted.
