# Monetization Readiness

## Goal

Germany Travel Checker should eventually make money, but not by damaging trust.

The product should first become useful, specific and credible for English-speaking visitors to Germany.

## Core Principle

Monetization should appear only where it helps solve the user's current travel trouble.

Bad monetization:

- random ads
- aggressive popups
- affiliate links before the user trusts the result
- unrelated travel blog offers
- anything near emergency medical guidance
- anything that makes the product feel less careful

Good monetization:

- shown after a relevant warning
- clearly separate from rule-based guidance
- useful for the user's situation
- easy to ignore
- never pretending to be official advice

## Strong Future Monetization Surfaces

### Trip Dates result

Possible later surfaces:

- eSIM
- travel insurance
- airport transfer
- luggage storage
- city passes
- public transport or train planning partners

Best timing:

- after the user sees a Sunday, public holiday or school holiday warning
- after a trip has medium or high risk
- after the user checks a city/date range

### Multi-city result

Possible later surfaces:

- luggage storage
- train planning
- travel insurance
- eSIM
- airport transfer
- flexible booking tools

Best timing:

- after transfer-day risk
- after non-low-risk segment warnings
- when the user has multiple cities and likely needs logistics help

### Arrival Trouble Check

Possible later surfaces:

- eSIM
- airport transfer
- luggage storage
- hotel early check-in guidance
- public transport passes
- travel insurance

Best timing:

- late-night or early-morning arrival
- Sunday or public holiday arrival
- long gap before hotel check-in

### City Guides

Possible later surfaces:

- luggage storage
- city passes
- airport transfers
- eSIM
- practical local travel products

Best timing:

- after useful city-specific guidance
- not before the guide earns trust

## Areas to Avoid

Do not monetize directly around:

- medical emergencies
- emergency pharmacy guidance
- police or emergency phone numbers
- live pharmacy duty schedules
- any text that mentions life-threatening situations
- safety-critical instructions

Emergency and medical content must stay clean, careful and non-commercial.

## Ads

Ads may be tested later only if:

- the product has enough useful content
- they do not interrupt checker results
- they do not appear inside emergency/safety guidance
- they do not make the site feel spammy
- they do not slow the page too much

Initial ad testing should be conservative.

## Affiliate Links

Affiliate links should be:

- clearly relevant
- disclosed where required
- placed after helpful guidance, not before it
- tested slowly
- removed if they reduce trust or usability

Do not add affiliate links to every page just because they are available.

## Short-term Revenue Readiness Checklist

Before adding monetization, check:

- homepage clearly explains the product
- Check Today feels useful and human
- planner results are stable
- city guides are helpful without being generic
- sitemap and indexable pages work
- basic production QA passes
- copy does not overpromise exact availability
- emergency guidance remains non-commercial

## Decision

For now, prioritize trust and usefulness.

The first monetization experiments should be small, relevant and result-aware, not generic.
