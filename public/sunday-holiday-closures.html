Sunday / public holiday trouble guide

Why are shops closed in Germany today?

If you are in Germany and supermarkets, shops or pharmacies suddenly seem closed, this guide helps you understand what is probably happening and what to do next.

Germany Travel Checker does not promise live opening hours. Use this page to understand the situation, then verify with official sources before you walk across town, book transport or change plans.

If you need something right now

Check whether it is Sunday or a public holiday.

Many regular shops and supermarkets in Germany are closed on Sundays and public holidays. Do not assume the closest supermarket is open just because Google shows a listing.

  • For food or water, check train stations, airports, gas stations or small convenience stores.
  • For medicine, look for an emergency pharmacy service, called Notdienst-Apotheke.
  • For baby items or urgent supplies, search inside major train stations or airports first.
  • Before walking across the city, verify the opening hours on Google Maps and the official business website.

Common needs

If you need something now, search by need first.

When you are tired, carrying luggage or traveling with children, do not start by walking to another normal supermarket. Choose the urgent need, then check the closest realistic fallback.

Water or snacks

Check train stations, airports, gas stations, kiosks, bakeries, cafés, restaurants or your hotel reception before crossing town.

Medicine

Search for the official Notdienst-Apotheke for your city or postcode. A normal pharmacy listing may not show the current duty pharmacy.

Baby supplies

Try larger stations, airports, gas stations or open drugstore-style locations first. Verify exact opening hours before you move.

A proper meal

Restaurants, cafés, hotel restaurants, station food courts and airport food areas may still be realistic options when retail shops are closed.

Verify before walking

If Google Maps says open but the door is closed

Treat Google Maps as a useful clue, not a guarantee. Holiday hours, Sunday exceptions and small-business updates can be wrong or missing.

Before you walk across the city with bags or kids, check the official business website, recent reviews, station or airport directories, or call the location if it matters.

Problem

The supermarket is closed

You arrived in Germany and the supermarket, drugstore or shopping street is closed, even though it is daytime.

Meaning

It may be Sunday or a public holiday

In many parts of Germany, normal retail shopping is restricted on Sundays and public holidays. Tourist areas, stations and airports may have exceptions.

What to do

Look for exception locations

Try larger train stations, airports, gas stations, kiosks or restaurants. For medicine, search for the local emergency pharmacy service.

Verify

Check before you move

  • Google Maps opening hours
  • Official business website
  • Official Notdienst-Apotheke search
  • Germany Travel Checker Check Today or Planner for holiday context

Important distinction

This guide explains the situation. It is not live availability.

A public holiday can explain why many places are closed, but it does not prove that a specific shop, pharmacy or restaurant is open or closed. Always verify the exact location before you walk, book transport or change your plan.
