Train trouble guide

German train words that can save your connection.

If you are carrying luggage, holding a child’s hand or trying to catch a connection, German station words can feel stressful. This guide explains common train disruption words in plain English and tells you what to check next.

At the station right now

Check the sign before you move.

Use this page to quickly understand what the German sign, app message or station announcement probably means before you leave the platform area.

  • Check the train number first. Do not rely only on the destination name.
  • Look at the latest platform board. Platforms can change shortly before departure.
  • If you see SEV, look for replacement bus signs before leaving the station area.
  • If your connection looks impossible, check DB Navigator, station displays or staff before changing plans.

Train problem happening now

If your train problem is happening now, identify the situation first.

A translation can tell you what a German word says. The important part is what it means for your next move: stay on the platform, change platforms, look for a bus, or verify your ticket options.

Platform changed

Check the train number first, then the latest station board. Do not move based only on the destination name.

Train cancelled

Check DB Navigator and station displays before boarding another train. Your ticket options may depend on the disruption and ticket type.

SEV / replacement bus

Do not wait only on the platform. Look for yellow SEV or Ersatzverkehr signs and check whether the bus leaves outside the station.

Connection looks impossible

Verify the delay, missed connection and next route before changing plans. Keep screenshots if your onward trip is affected.

Verify before you move

Do not rely on one screen only.

DB Navigator is useful, but station displays, platform signs and staff can matter when a platform changes or a replacement bus starts outside the station.

Germany Travel Checker does not provide live train status. Use this page to understand the situation, then verify with official sources before you leave the platform area, board another train or walk to a bus stop.

Gleisänderung

Platform change

Your train may leave from a different platform than originally shown. Check the train number, not only the destination, and look at the latest station display before boarding.

fällt aus

Cancelled train

The train is cancelled. You may need another train, route or connection. Check DB Navigator, station displays or staff before leaving the station area.

SEV / Schienenersatzverkehr

Replacement bus

Part of the rail route may be replaced by a bus. Look for signs saying SEV, Ersatzverkehr or Bus. Allow extra time, especially with luggage, children or a tight connection.

Bauarbeiten

Construction works

Construction works can change routes, platforms, travel times or stopping patterns. Check your route again on the day of travel and leave more transfer time than usual.

Umleitung

Detour or rerouting

Your route may be diverted. The train or local transport may still run, but not exactly as planned. Check whether your stop, platform or connection has changed.

Verspätung

Delay

The train is delayed. If you have a connection, check whether the next train is still reachable. Do not rely only on the printed plan or an old screenshot.

What to do first

When things change suddenly

  • Check the train number, not only the destination.
  • Look at the latest station display before boarding.
  • Use DB Navigator or the local transport app to verify the route.
  • Leave extra time if you have luggage, children or a tight connection.
  • If you see SEV, look for replacement bus signs before leaving the area.

Germany Travel Checker does not provide live train disruption data. Train routes, platforms and replacement transport can change quickly. Always verify current details with DB Navigator, the local transport operator, station displays or staff.
